# Prompting

Good prompts are not about technical wizardry. They are about telling AI clearly what you want, who it is for, and how you want it back. This section gives you a few simple frameworks that make that easier when the task is complex.

---

## Why prompting matters

Modern AI understands what you mean, not just what you write. Casual prompts work fine for most things. But when output needs to be consistent, when others will repeat the same task, or when the result really matters, structure helps.

Think of the models below like a spirit level. You do not use one every time you hang a picture. But when it has to be straight, you are glad it is in the drawer.

---

## A note on technique vs communication

It is tempting to treat prompting as a technical skill. It is not. It is a communication skill.

If you can brief a colleague clearly, you can prompt AI clearly. The models in this section are not magic incantations. They are reminders to do what good communicators already do: be specific about who, what, why, and how.

The best prompt is not the most elaborate one. It is the one that gives AI just enough to deliver what you actually wanted.
